# Vision

**Phase:** 1 — Product Documentation
**Status:** Draft for review

---

## Vision Statement

> **A world where people feel time instead of losing it — where the present
> moment is vivid enough that starting becomes natural.**

Wake exists to end the quiet tragedy of postponed lives: not the dramatic
failures, but the ordinary evenings where a day passed unfelt and the thing
that mattered never began.

## The Category We Are Creating

Wake is a **time awareness product** — the first of its category.

It is deliberately *not* positioned as a productivity app, a todo app, a
habit tracker, or an anti-procrastination tool. Those categories share an
assumption we reject: that people fail to act because their work is
insufficiently organized or their discipline insufficiently enforced. The
Phase 0 research (`../research/ProcrastinationScience.md`) shows the real
levers are perceptual and emotional: time passes invisibly, futures feel
unreal, and starting feels worse in anticipation than in fact.

A time awareness product works on those levers directly:

- it renders the passage of *today* as something seen, heard, and felt;
- it makes the future feel near by making the present feel finite;
- it converts each moment of awareness into the smallest possible act of
  starting.

"Anti-procrastination" is the outcome our users get. "Time awareness" is
what the product *is*. This distinction governs all naming, store
positioning, and copy (ratified in the Phase 0 review; see
`../research/ProductOpportunityReport.md` §1).

## What the World Looks Like If We Succeed

- A glance at a phone's home screen carries the same gentle information a
  sundial once did: the day has a shape, and you are somewhere inside it.
- "I'll just start for two minutes" replaces "I'll do it later" as the
  culturally obvious move — the way "put on your running shoes" became
  shorthand for beginning exercise.
- People who have spent years in the delay–guilt–anxiety cycle experience
  something most productivity tools never gave them: a tool that never once
  made them feel worse about themselves.
- Users need Wake *less* over time — felt time and small starts become
  internalized habits. Graduation is a success story, not churn to be
  fought.

## Vision Horizons

- **Near (MVP):** One phone, one widget, one button, one chosen voice. The
  core loop proves that felt time plus tiny starts moves real behavior
  (instrumented per P12).
- **Mid:** Time awareness spreads across the surfaces where time is
  actually lost — lock screens, watches, ambient audio — while the product
  itself stays one loop deep. Voices grow as a considered set (including
  the optional Stoic philosophy pack), never as a content feed.
- **Far:** "Time awareness" is a recognized product category with Wake as
  its reference point, the way "focus timer" and "habit tracker" are
  categories today — and the category's norms (no shame, no ledgers, no
  engagement traps) are the ones we set.

## What This Vision Explicitly Refuses

- To measure a life in productivity metrics.
- To trade user attention for revenue.
- To motivate through fear, shame, or manufactured urgency.
- To become a system that must itself be managed.

The constitution (`ProductPrinciples.md`) makes these refusals binding.

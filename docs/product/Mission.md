# Mission

**Phase:** 1 — Product Documentation
**Status:** Draft for review

## Mission Statement

> **Wake makes time felt and starting small: an ambient sense of today,
> a chosen voice, and one button that turns awareness into two minutes of
> real action.**

## The Mission, Unpacked

**"Makes time felt"** — We build the perceptual layer: a home-screen shape
of the day, granular time language ("6h 40m of today left"), and optionally
a spoken clock. Time stops being a coordinate you look up and becomes a
current you stand in. (Pillar I.)

**"Starting small"** — We build the behavioral layer: a single Start button
that begins a two-minute commitment, honored honestly. Small enough to slip
under resistance; real enough to change how the task feels. (Pillar II.)

**"A chosen voice"** — We build the emotional layer: the user decides who
talks to them about time — a challenging voice or a nurturing one — under a
contract that challenges behavior and never the person. The same care
extends to the moment users need it most: coming back after a lapse, with
zero judgment.

## How We Work (operating commitments)

1. **Evidence first.** Features operationalize documented mechanisms from
   the Phase 0 research; unvalidated bets are instrumented and allowed to
   lose (P12).
2. **Restraint as strategy.** One loop, few words, no feature that exists
   because it was easy (P13, P7).
3. **The user's exit is our win.** We measure starts into real life, not
   time in app (P5).
4. **Safety before edge.** No shame, no dark patterns, no visible debt —
   even when they would convert better (P3, P4, P8).
5. **Private by architecture.** Local-first, minimal data, no account for
   core features (P11).
6. **Accessible by premise.** Time must be feelable through sight, sound,
   and touch (P10).

## Who We Serve

Ambitious people who already know what they should be doing — students,
developers, designers, creators, professionals, founders — and who are
tired of tools that organize their guilt instead of dissolving the wall
between intention and action. Detailed in `TargetAudience.md` and
`Personas.md`.
